THE HERITAGE EXPERIENCE

More Than
A Race.

Heritage Run 4 brings together runners, families, communities, and the spirit of Iloilo in one unforgettable race experience.

The Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon – Heritage Run 4, organized by Velocity 5000, is a road running event that celebrates movement, culture, and the heritage of Iloilo City.

Inspired by the vibrant spirit of the Dinagyang Festival, the event invites runners to experience Iloilo through its streets, landmarks, communities, and unmistakable Ilonggo hospitality.

RUNNER'S GUIDE

Everything You
Need To Know.

From registration and race kits to race-day essentials, find answers to the questions runners ask most.

BEFORE YOU RUN

Prepare.
Then Run.

Keep this guide handy as you prepare for Heritage Run 4.
01 How do I register?

Registration is available exclusively through our official Veent Registration Portal. Simply complete the online registration, select your preferred payment method, and follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your slot.

02 Until when can I register?

Online registration is open until November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM.

We encourage all participants to register early to enjoy available promotional rates and to allow sufficient time for race preparations.

03 What race distances are available?

Heritage Run 4 offers four race categories:

  • 5K Fun Run
  • 10K Road Race
  • 21K Half Marathon
  • 42K Full Marathon
04 What is included in my registration?

Your registration includes:

  • Official Race Bib
  • Official Event Singlet
  • Sponsor Giveaways, if available

Finisher Medals and Finisher Shirts (eligible categories) are awarded only upon successfully completing the race.

05 Where are the Start and Finish Lines?

The official Start and Finish venue will be announced at a later date. Please follow our official Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon Facebook page and regularly visit this website for updates.

06 When and where is Race Kit Claiming?

Race Kit Claiming will be held from January 13–16, 2027.

The complete venue and claiming guidelines will be announced through our official Facebook page and website.

07 Can someone else claim my race kit?

Yes. Your representative must present:

  • Signed Authorization Letter
  • Copy of your Valid ID
  • Representative's Valid ID
  • Registration Confirmation
08 Can I transfer my registration to another runner?

Registration transfers may be allowed before the announced deadline, subject to organizer approval. Applicable fees may apply.

09 Can I change my race category?

Category changes are subject to slot availability and may require payment of the registration fee difference. Requests beyond the announced deadline cannot be accommodated.

10 Can I cancel my registration?

Registration fees are non-refundable. Please ensure that you have selected the correct race category before completing your registration.

11 Will there be hydration and medical stations?

Yes. Hydration stations, medical teams, ambulances, and emergency responders will be available throughout the race course.

12 Will there be baggage deposit?

Yes. A baggage deposit area will be available near the Start/Finish Area for registered participants.

The organizer is not responsible for valuables placed inside baggage.

13 Will all finishers receive a medal?

Yes. All official finishers who complete the race within the prescribed rules and cutoff time will receive an Official Finisher Medal.

14 When will the official race route be released?

The official race routes and course maps will be published on our website and official Facebook page prior to race day.

15 Where can I stay if I'm coming from outside Iloilo?

We recommend staying within Iloilo City Proper for convenient access to the race venue, restaurants, and transportation.

Recommended partner hotels will be announced soon.

16 Can foreign runners join?

Absolutely! Heritage Run 4 welcomes runners of all nationalities to experience Iloilo City's premier marathon event.

17 Where can I find official event updates?

Follow the official Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon Facebook page and visit this website regularly for announcements, schedules, race guides, route maps, and other important event updates.

STAY UPDATED

Event schedules, race instructions, venue announcements, and other important updates will be published through our official channels.
