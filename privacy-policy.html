Privacy Policy

The Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon – Heritage Run 4, organized by Velocity 5000, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or use our participant services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include your name, email address, contact details, registration information, race category, distance, and other information necessary for event administration.

Event registration information is collected and managed through our third-party registration provider. We may receive and process relevant registration information for participant verification, communication, and race administration.

Registration Verification

Our website provides a registration verification service that allows participants to confirm their event registration.

To perform a verification search, participants may be asked to provide the First Name, Last Name, and Email Address used during registration.

These details are used to locate matching registration records. Verification results do not display the participant's email address or other private registration information.

How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Process and administer event registration.
  • Verify registration and participant status.
  • Communicate important race and event information.
  • Provide participant services and race-day assistance.
  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Administer event operations, results, and related services.

Protection of Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Personal information is handled only for legitimate and appropriate purposes related to the event and our services.

Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality and understand how visitors use our website.

We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated information about website usage. Analytics is enabled only after a visitor accepts our website's privacy notice.

Third-Party Services

Certain services, including event registration and website analytics, may be provided through third-party platforms.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), data subjects have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to be informed, access, correction, objection, and other rights provided by applicable law.
